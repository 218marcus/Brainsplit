🧠 Brainsplit Guide

🎯 Goal

Control both brains, maintain score and handle random events; any side fails = Game Over.

🖱️ Controls

  • Left Brain Press A / S to answer (initial 9s, -1s per 400pts, min 5s)
  • Right Brain Press ← / → or swipe to control paddle
  • Mobile/tablet: tap left buttons, swipe right to control paddle

🏆 Scoring

  • Left correct: +50 pts
  • Right catch: +10 pts
  • No extra survival bonus

⚡ Random Events (every 400 pts)

  • +1 ball (max 6)
  • Swap sides (2s delay)
  • Number sort (5s, +50 if correct, wrong/timeout = Game Over)
  • Gravity flip (paddle to top, miss top = lose)

💡 Special

Normal mode: bottom lose; Flip mode: top lose. Both top/bottom walls bounce (top in normal, bottom in flip).
